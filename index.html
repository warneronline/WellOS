Week 1 — what to expect
Day 1 is often easier than expected. Days 2–3: fatigue, headaches, feeling "off" — your body realising sugar isn't coming. This is normal. Days 4–6: cravings easing, energy stabilising. Day 7+: clearer thinking, cravings near-gone, sleep improving.

How to export from Garmin Connect
1
🏃 Activities (runs, swims, walks)
Open Garmin Connect app → Activities → top-right menu → Export to CSV. Or on desktop: connect.garmin.com → Activities → scroll to bottom → Export CSV. Gives you one row per activity with date, type, distance, duration, HR, pace.
2
😴 Sleep data
On desktop: connect.garmin.com → Health Stats → Sleep → choose date range → Export (top right). Gives you date, total sleep hours, deep/light/REM/awake split, sleep score.
3
👣 Steps & daily wellness
On desktop: connect.garmin.com → Health Stats → Steps → date range → Export. Gives daily steps, active minutes, floors, calories. Do this weekly — takes 30 seconds.
NOTE
Garmin's CSV column names vary by device and activity type. This importer matches columns by name, so it handles that automatically. If a column isn't found in your export, that field simply won't appear — nothing will break.
